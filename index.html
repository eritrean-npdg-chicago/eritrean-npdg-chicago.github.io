About us

The Eritrean National Public Diplomacy Group (NPDG) in Chicago is a volunteer group of Eritrean Americans in the Chicago area. We work to strengthen understanding of Eritrea among state and federal officials, policymakers, and the wider public.

Our focus is direct engagement: meeting with elected representatives, sharing accurate information about Eritrea, and making sure the Eritrean American community has a voice in conversations that affect it.

Mission and goals

To build lasting relationships between the Eritrean American community and the officials who represent it, and to make sure decisions affecting Eritrea and its people are informed by accurate information and community voices.

Our goals

Build relationships with officials

Hold regular meetings with Illinois members of Congress, state legislators, and candidates, and keep those relationships going between elections.

Bring concrete, policy-specific asks

Back every meeting with written briefs on the issues our community faces, including financial access for families and businesses.

Show up in civic life

Volunteer at community and civic events, learn from established organizations, and bring officials into Eritrean community celebrations.

What we've done

Since fall 2025, our members have met with federal and state offices, volunteered at civic events, and brought officials into community celebrations.

2026
  • January
    Meeting with Rep. Jan Schakowsky

    Met directly with the Congresswoman in Skokie on the diaspora community and financial access barriers facing Eritrean American families. She asked for a written brief and coalition connections.

  • April
    Meeting with State Sen. Lakesia Collins's office

    Introduced NPDG Chicago to the Senator's staff and offered volunteers for her community events.

  • May
    Volunteered at Sen. Collins's Mother's Day event, Douglas Park

    Our members volunteered at the event and met the Senator's team.

  • May
    Skokie Festival of Cultures

    Represented the Eritrean community at Skokie's annual cultural festival.

  • May
    Eritrean Independence Day celebration, Chicago

    Welcomed Sen. Collins's office to the community celebration. Rep. Schakowsky's office sent a written statement recognizing the day.

  • July
    Guidance from the Chinese American Service League (CASL)

    Met with one of Chicago's largest community organizations, which has advised us on structuring and building our organization. Followed up in September.

  • August
    Introductory call with IL-09 nominee Daniel Biss

    Introduced the group and the community's priorities.
