Get to know Eritrea

Eritrea at a glance

A young country with an ancient history, Eritrea sits on the Red Sea in the Horn of Africa. It is home to nine ethnic groups, a modernist capital recognized by UNESCO, and more than a thousand kilometers of coastline.

People and culture

Eritrea officially recognizes nine ethnic groups, each with its own language and traditions. Christianity and Islam are both long established, and many towns have churches and mosques side by side.

Coffee ceremony

Beans are roasted by hand, ground, and brewed in a clay pot called a jebena. Guests are served three rounds, and turning one down is rare.

Food

Meals are shared from one plate. Injera, a spongy flatbread, is served with stews like tsebhi and shiro, a spiced chickpea dish.

Cycling

Cycling is the national passion, and Asmara hosts races through its streets. In 2024, Biniam Girmay became the first Black African rider to win a stage of the Tour de France.

Architecture

Asmara's 1930s modernist buildings, from cinemas to a gas station shaped like an airplane, earned the city a place on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 2017.
